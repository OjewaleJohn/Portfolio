# Financial Performance & Investment Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses financial and operational data across 50 U.S. states. The dataset includes R&D expenditure, marketing expenditure, campaign activity, profit, employee count and product count.

## Business Goal
The goal was to evaluate financial performance and resource efficiency across markets and support management decisions around spending, budgeting and business planning.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- Calculated fields
- Excel formulas
- Charts and dashboard reporting

## Analysis Performed
- Compared profit performance across states.
- Analysed R&D and marketing expenditure.
- Compared investment levels with profit outcomes.
- Calculated operational-efficiency measures such as profit per employee and profit per product.
- Reviewed campaign activity alongside financial performance.
- Built PivotTables and dashboards to compare geographic performance.
- Identified high- and low-performing markets.
- Analysed relationships between business spending and profitability.

## Key Measures
The workbook contains approximately:
- $7.33M in R&D spending
- $5.55M in marketing spending
- $2.99M in profit
- 10,400 employees
- 1,880 products

## Metric Interpretation
Because the dataset does not contain revenue, ratios based on profit divided by marketing or R&D spending should be described as investment-efficiency or profit-to-spend measures rather than conventional profit margin.

Examples:
- Profit-to-Marketing-Spend Ratio
- Profit-to-R&D-Spend Ratio
- Profit per Employee
- Profit per Product

## Dashboard Quality Improvements
Before publishing the workbook as a final portfolio project:
- Remove Grand Total from chart source ranges where it appears as a separate category.
- Standardise currency formatting.
- Rename financial ratios so they accurately describe the underlying calculations.

## Business Value
This project shows how financial and operational data can be converted into management-ready insights to support budgeting, resource allocation, market comparison and business planning.
