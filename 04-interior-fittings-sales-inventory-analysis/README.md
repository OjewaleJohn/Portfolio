## Project Overview
This is a synthetic end-to-end MySQL analytics project designed around an interior-design and fittings business. It was created to demonstrate how sales, customer, inventory, procurement, budgeting and operational data can be integrated for management analysis.

> Note: The dataset is synthetic and was created for portfolio and learning purposes.

## Business Goal
The project was designed to answer business questions relevant to a retail and interior-fittings operation, including:
- How are sales and gross profit changing over time?
- Which products and categories perform best?
- Which customers contribute the most value?
- Which products are at risk of stockout or overstock?
- When should stock be reordered?
- Which suppliers perform best?
- How does actual performance compare with budget?
- What demand level should management plan for next?
