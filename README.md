## 05 — Quantitative Content Analysis of Human Trafficking Coverage

**Research question:** How frequently and prominently did The Punch and The Guardian newspapers report human trafficking, and what reporting patterns were present?

This undergraduate research project provides evidence of my quantitative analytical foundation during university.

### Data scope

- **Study period:** 2019–2020
- **Population:** 1,462 newspaper issues
- **Sample:** 400 newspaper issues
- **Relevant human-trafficking items identified:** 282
- **Publications:** The Punch and The Guardian

### Analysis performed

- Structured content coding and classification
- Simple random sampling
- Frequency and percentage analysis
- Cross-tabulation
- Comparison of newspaper coverage
- Story-prominence analysis
- Journalistic-genre analysis
- Direction-of-coverage analysis
- Pattern identification and research interpretation

### Selected findings

- The Punch accounted for **62.8%** of the identified stories, compared with **37.2%** for The Guardian.
- **86.5%** of the stories appeared on inside pages.
- Straight news represented **68.1%** of the journalistic formats analysed.
- Trafficking for forced criminal activities represented **50.7%** of the coded trafficking categories.

### Analytical workflow

**Research question → sampling → data collection → coding → categorisation → analysis → comparison → interpretation → recommendation**

The repository contains a portfolio-friendly README, structured results data and selected evidence from the original academic research rather than the full thesis.

# How I Approach an Analytics Problem

My preferred analytical workflow is:

1. **Understand the business or research question**
2. **Identify the required data**
3. **Inspect and validate the data**
4. **Clean and structure the dataset**
5. **Define relevant KPIs or analytical variables**
6. **Analyse trends, relationships and performance**
7. **Visualise or summarise the findings**
8. **Interpret what the results mean**
9. **Communicate practical recommendations**
10. **Automate repeatable reporting where appropriate**

The goal is not simply to produce charts or queries. It is to make the data useful for a decision.
